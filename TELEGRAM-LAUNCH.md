## 二、首批 7 条内容（发"真东西"，一周的量）

> 每条都是可核实的数字或方法，不是观点。这是和满街"币圈交流群"的区别。

### 1. 你每年被抽走多少
```
The average cost of sending money home is 6.2% worldwide (World Bank).

On $200/month that's $274/year — gone before it arrives.

Sub-Saharan Africa: over 8%.
The UN target is 3% by 2030. Nobody is close.

Where does it go, and how do you pay less? Ask below.
```

### 2. 银行凭什么收 11%
```
Why a bank wire costs ~11.4%:

Your money hops through SWIFT intermediaries. Each one
takes a cut, and you see none of it on your receipt.
The fee is itemised as "$25 wire fee" — the rest is hidden
in the exchange rate.

Digital transfers skip the chain. That's the whole difference.
```

### 3. 「零手续费」是怎么骗人的
```
"ZERO FEE!" usually means the cost moved into the exchange rate.

How to check:
1. Look up the mid-market rate (xe.com, or open.er-api.com)
2. Compare with the rate your provider gives you
3. The gap IS the fee.

A "0% fee" provider giving you a rate 3% worse than mid-market
is charging you 3%. Same as everywhere else, just quieter.
```

### 4. P2P 到底是什么（诚实版）
```
Exchange P2P is often the cheapest route — around 1–2%.

But be clear-eyed about it:
• You need a counterparty who will take your money
• You carry the counterparty risk yourself
• In some countries merchants get their accounts frozen by risk checks
• It's not a licensed service. If it goes wrong, you have no recourse.

Cheap ≠ free. Suited to people who can judge that risk.
```

### 5. 自己算你的真实成本（方法）
```
The formula. Use it on any provider.

Total cost % = (fee / amount) + ((mid-rate − your rate) / mid-rate)

Example: sending $200
  Fee $4                    → 2.0%
  Mid-rate 1000, you get 975 → 2.5%
  Your real cost            → 4.5%

Now compare every option you have with the same formula.
```

### 6. 骗局识别
```
Scam patterns targeting people sending money home:

✗ "Send to this account, I'll transfer it for you, no fee"
✗ Extra-low rate only available via DM
✗ App not listed on the official site of the company
✗ Anyone asking you to pay a "release fee" to receive funds
✗ Groups where every post is someone's profit screenshot

If they contact YOU first with a deal — that's the tell.
```

### 7. 从哪条渠道开始（转化贴，克制）
```
Most people here start with the channel they grew up with, then
never check again. Prices have moved a lot.

Order to check, cheapest first:
1. Exchange P2P (cheapest, some risk — see post #4)
2. Licensed digital transfer
3. Mobile wallet
4. Bank / agent

Remittance accounts at exchanges are free to open and you don't
have to trade anything to use the transfer side.

Calculator: remitcost.pages.dev
```

## 四、诚实边界（必须守住）

- 报价随时间和渠道变，**说清哪条走廊、什么时间**，不要给"通用最低价"
- 加密资产有价格与法规风险，**不要暗示"更便宜所以无风险"**
- P2P 的风险必须每次都说，不能只讲便宜
- 不承诺收益、不晒收益截图 —— 这是和骗子群的分界线

**为什么这条线重要**：这个群的信任就是资产。满街都是返佣群，
**唯一能区别于它们的就是"我说的数我自己能核"**。
